# Quiz pool choice and familiar faces

## Why
Sara (2026-11-22) still met too many people she had never heard of in Who Is This?, and
Guess the Song was the hardest game (best 50%). She asked for familiar faces only, a
Korean / not Korean choice, and songs from real top charts that can be played per period.

## What changes
- Faces are ranked by Wikipedia page views (English + Korean, 60 days). Played: the 600
  best-known non-Korean faces and 400 best-known Korean faces ("Medium with more Koreans"),
  plus the original deck, one-name stars, K-pop groups and their members. Others get `rare`.
- K-pop members show their group on reveal ("BLACKPINK").
- Songs come from Billboard year-end top 12 (1980-2025), Korea's Circle/Gaon number ones
  (2010-2025), hand-picked Korean hits before 2010, the earlier favourites and Sara's playlist.
  The weekly store charts are dropped. Each song carries the year it was a hit.
- Before Who Is This? and Guess the Song a picker asks Mix / Korean / Not Korean (Pop), and
  for songs All / 80s-90s / 2000s / 2010s-now. The last choice is remembered on the phone.
